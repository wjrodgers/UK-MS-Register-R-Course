SYNTHETIC MS PARTICIPANTS AND EDSS - DATA DICTIONARY

No records describe real people. The user's 42-row summary supplies targets
for subgroup age means and SDs. Counts and individual-level correlations were
not supplied. The agreed simulation uses 50 people per subgroup: 2,100 total.
Equal subgroup sizes do not reproduce the prevalence of gender or MS types in
the original population. Results pooled across groups depend on this choice.

Participants.csv - one row per person (2,100 rows)
UserId: unique integer ID linking to Scores.csv.
Site: artificial North/South label, allocated cyclically, with inconsistent
      capitalisation deliberately retained for the string-cleaning exercise.
Gender: Female, Male, PNTS, preserving the supplied labels. PNTS is interpreted
        here as prefer not to say; check its meaning in the source dictionary.
ms_at_diagnosis: PPMS, RRMS, SPMS, Unknown, exactly as supplied.
ms_type_now: Benign, PPMS, RRMS, SPMS, Unknown, exactly as supplied.
age: synthetic age in years at 2025-12-31, rounded to two decimals.
age_at_diagnosis: synthetic age at diagnosis in years.
age_at_msnow: synthetic age associated with recording/classifying current MS
              type; this field's precise original meaning was not supplied.

The three age variables reproduce each supplied mean and sample SD within
0.01 years. Within-person positive association is a simulation assumption.
The generator uses shared bounded random variation with additional variation
for the two historical ages, then centres/scales to match the supplied moments.
It accepts adult current ages from 18 to 115 and historical ages from 0 to
current age. It does not impose normal distributions or recover real people.
The high mean age (93) for Male/SPMS/Benign is retained as supplied.

Do not interpret all combinations of diagnosis/current MS labels as confirmed
clinical transitions. The labels (including Benign and Unknown) are retained
for reproducing the table, not reclassified into a clinical taxonomy.
Unknown is an explicit source category, not an NA value.

Age ordering
The PNTS/RRMS/RRMS target has mean age_at_msnow 28.8 versus diagnosis age 29.2.
It is impossible to preserve both means while requiring every msnow age to be
at least the diagnosis age. We preserve the targets and flag individual rows
where age_at_msnow < age_at_diagnosis (94 rows across the generated data).
These flags ask for clarification of source meaning; they are not automatically
corrected or excluded. Both historical ages are no greater than current age.

Scores.csv - one row per synthetic EDSS assessment record (3151 rows)
RecordId: unique integer record identifier; larger ID breaks a same-date tie.
UserId: participant link. ID 9999 deliberately has no enrolled participant.
CompletedDate: YYYY-MM-DD. Blank = unknown; cut-off is 2025-12-31.
EDSS: simulated Expanded Disability Status Scale value. This ordinal scale
has categories 0 and 1 to 10 in half steps; there is no 0.5 category. Higher
values indicate greater disability, but equal numeric steps do not represent
equal changes in disability. The generated living cohort uses 0 and 1 to 9.5;
10 (death due to MS) is not generated. No neurological examinations are implied.
EDSS reference: https://mstrust.org.uk/a-z/expanded-disability-status-scale-edss

User-requested contrast follows CURRENT MS type, not type at diagnosis:
Latent mean targets: Benign 1.5, PPMS 6, RRMS 5, SPMS 3.5, Unknown 2.5.
Values are drawn with SD 1 and mapped to allowed categories. Rounding/bounds,
missingness and record selection mean achieved means differ from these targets.
This imposed ordering is a teaching scenario, not a claim about typical MS
populations. In particular, the ordering of RRMS and SPMS is deliberately set
by the request. Report medians/quartiles and counts when exploring EDSS; means
are also exported to verify the requested simulation pattern.

Deliberate teaching problems
About 5% of latest EDSS values are missing, plus the special cases below.
UserId 5: latest score missing despite an earlier assessment.
UserId 6: latest raw score is 99, deliberately invalid, despite an earlier score.
UserId 8: same-date tie; larger RecordId selects EDSS 2.
UserId 10: only assessment is after the cut-off.
UserId 11: only assessment has missing date.
UserId 12: no assessment record.
UserId 9999: unmatched assessment record.

Cleaning and outputs
Script 3 changes invalid EDSS to NA and retains raw files unchanged. It flags
three score/date review records (UserIds 6, 10, 11). It selects the latest dated
record on/before the cut-off, keeping a missing latest score as missing. It
left-joins onto participants, so all 2,100 people remain. There are 2,097 people
with eligible records, 2,014 observed EDSS scores and 86 missing EDSS values.
Of those 86, three have no eligible record and 83 have a record with missing EDSS.

Output/AnalysisData.csv is the cleaned one-row-per-person file INCLUDING EDSS.
Output/Scores_clean.csv contains all assessment rows with invalid EDSS set to
NA and date/score flags retained; it still contains unmatched/ineligible rows.
Use AnalysisData for the participant-level exercise, not all assessment rows.
Output/ReviewRecords.csv and UnmatchedScores.csv preserve the review evidence.
Output/AgeOrderReview.csv contains the age-order flags for discussion.
Output/Subgroup comparison.csv shows targets, achieved statistics and differences.
Output/GroupSummary.csv describes latest EDSS by current MS type.

Reproduction
Instructor/Generate data.R uses set.seed(20260907). It deliberately overwrites
the two teaching CSVs when run from the project. It is not needed during class.
Instructor/Verify course.R rebuilds the analysis and checks targets, EDSS values,
missingness, joins and the requested mean ordering. All these checks pass.
